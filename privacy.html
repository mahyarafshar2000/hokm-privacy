Hokm: privacy policy

Effective date: October 2026

This policy covers the Hokm card game app for Android ("the app").

What the app itself collects

The app has no accounts and does not collect, store or send your name or any other personal information to the developer. Player names you type on the start screen stay on your device and are not saved after you close the app. The app has no analytics. Your sound settings and whether you bought the ad-free upgrade are stored only on your device.

Advertising

The free version shows ads served by Google AdMob: a full-screen ad when a game ends and a banner ad on the pause menu. To show and measure ads, Google AdMob may collect and use information such as your device's advertising ID, IP address, approximate location, device and app information, and how you interact with ads. This is handled by Google under its own privacy policy: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites. The developer does not receive this information.

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the app asks for your consent before showing ads that use personal data. You can change your choice at any time using "Ad privacy settings" on the pause menu, when it is shown. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android device settings.

Purchases

The one-time "Remove ads" purchase is processed by Google Play. The developer does not receive your payment details. The app only checks with Google Play whether the purchase was made.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from them.
